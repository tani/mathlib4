### Technical Metadata Brief

#### 1. **Key Definitions & Theorems**

| Name | Type / Signature | Purpose |
|------|------------------|---------|
| `exists_isNilpotent_isSemisimple_of_separable_of_dvd_pow` | `∀ {P : K[X]} {k : ℕ}, P.Separable → minpoly K f ∣ P^k → ∃ᵉ n s ∈ adjoin K {f}, IsNilpotent n ∧ IsSemisimple s ∧ f = n + s` | Core decomposition lemma: under separability and divisibility conditions, `f` splits into nilpotent + semisimple components lying in the subalgebra generated by `f`. |

| `IsNilpotent`, `IsSemisimple` | `End K V → Prop` | Standard properties of endomorphisms: nilpotent (`f^k = 0` for some `k`) and semisimple (diagonalizable over algebraic closure, or equivalently, minimal polynomial squarefree). |
| `aeval` | `K[X] → End K V → End K V` | Polynomial evaluation at an endomorphism (`P(f)`), used to construct components via functional calculus. |
| `adjoin K {f}` | Subalgebra of `End K V` generated by `f` | Ensures that the nilpotent and semisimple parts are *polynomial* in `f`. |

#### 2. **Naming Conventions**

- **Prefixes**:
  - `is_`: Denotes properties (e.g., `IsNilpotent`, `IsSemisimple`, `IsUnit`, `IsCoprime`).
  - `aeval`: Abbreviation for *algebra evaluation* (polynomial applied to an endomorphism).
  - `mem`: Used in membership proofs (e.g., `mem : s ∈ adjoin K {f}`).
- **Suffixes**:
  - `_of_`: Indicates assumptions or hypotheses used (e.g., `of_separable_of_dvd_pow`).
  - `_left`, `_right`: Often used in `IsCoprime`/`IsUnit` contexts (e.g., `sep.pow_left`).
- **Variables**:
  - `ff`, `P'`, `P'`: Local abbreviations (`ff := ⟨f, ...⟩`, `P' := derivative P`).
  - `sep`, `nil`, `nil'`, `sep'`: Abbreviations for separability and nilpotency hypotheses.

#### 3. **Tactic Stack**

- **Core tactics**:
  - `obtain ⟨...⟩`: To destruct existential/universal hypotheses.
  - `rw [← map_pow, Subtype.ext_iff]`, `simp [ff, hq]`: Rewriting and simplification using algebraic properties.
  - `refine ⟨..., ?_, ..., ?_⟩`: Constructing existentials with holes to fill later.
  - `rwa [...] at hss`: Rewrite + assumption.
  - `simpa [...] using h`: Simplify using a hypothesis.
  - `exact ...`: Final step of proof.
- **Domain-specific automation**:
  - `aesop` not present — proof is highly structured and manual.
  - Heavy use of `simp` with custom lemmas (`map_pow`, `map_add`, `map_mul`, `minpoly.dvd_iff`).
  - `ring` not used — arithmetic handled via `simp` and `rw`.

#### 4. **Proof Logic**

- **High-level strategy**:
  1. Reduce to the case where the minimal polynomial divides a power of a *separable* polynomial (via squarefree decomposition over perfect fields).
  2. Use Newton’s method (encoded in `existsUnique_nilpotent_sub_and_aeval_eq_zero`) to construct `s` such that:
     - `aeval s P = 0` ⇒ `s` is semisimple (via `isSemisimple_of_squarefree_aeval_eq_zero`).
     - `n := f - s` is nilpotent (via construction).
  3. Verify that both `n` and `s` lie in `adjoin K {f}` (closed under subtraction, contains `f`).
- **Key logical flow**:
  - Induction not used.
  - Relies on *existence-uniqueness* of nilpotent correction in a local algebra setting (`aeval ff P'` invertible ⇒ Newton iteration converges).
  - Uses algebraic facts: separability ⇒ squarefreeness over perfect fields; `minpoly ∣ P^k` ⇒ `aeval f P` nilpotent.

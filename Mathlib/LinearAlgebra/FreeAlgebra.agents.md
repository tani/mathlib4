### Technical Metadata Brief: `FreeAlgebra` Linear Algebra Properties

---

#### **1. Key Definitions & Theorems**

| Name | Type / Signature | Purpose |
|------|------------------|---------|
| `basisFreeMonoid` | `Basis (FreeMonoid X) R (FreeAlgebra R X)` | Constructs a basis of the free algebra over `R` indexed by `FreeMonoid X`, via the monoid algebra equivalence. Maps each word (as a `FreeMonoid X` element) to the corresponding monomial `1 • x₁ * ⋯ * xₙ`. |
| `Module.Free R (FreeAlgebra R X)` | Instance | Shows that the free algebra is a free module over `R`, induced by the basis `basisFreeMonoid`. |
| `rank_eq` | `Module.rank R (FreeAlgebra R X) = Cardinal.lift.{u} (Cardinal.mk (List X))` | Computes the rank (i.e., dimension) of `FreeAlgebra R X` as a module over `R`: it equals the cardinality of `List X`, lifted to the appropriate universe. |
| `Algebra.rank_adjoin_le` | `Module.rank R (adjoin R s) ≤ max #s ℵ₀` | Bounds the rank of the subalgebra generated (`adjoin`) by a set `s` in an `R`-algebra `S`: rank is at most the maximum of the cardinality of `s` and ℵ₀. |

- **Suffixes**:
  - `_eq_rank'`: used in lemmas connecting a basis to rank (e.g., `Basis.mk_eq_rank'`)

#### **4. Proof Logic**

- **`rank_eq` proof sketch**:
  1. Use `Basis.mk_eq_rank'` to relate the rank to the indexing type of a basis.
  2. Substitute `basisFreeMonoid` and simplify using `Cardinal.lift_id`.
  3. Apply `FreeMonoid`-related cardinal arithmetic (`FreeMonoid` is equivalent to `List X` up to cardinality).
- **`Algebra.rank_adjoin_le` proof sketch**:
  1. Rewrite `adjoin R s` as the range of `FreeAlgebra.lift`.
  2. Split into cases: `R` subsingleton or nontrivial.
     - Subsingleton case: use `rank_subsingleton`.
     - Nontrivial case: lift ranks to a common universe and apply `lift_rank_range_le`.
  3. Simplify using `FreeAlgebra.rank_eq`, cardinal arithmetic (`lift_id'`, `lift_umax`, `lift_le`, `max_comm`), and `mk_list_le_max`.

### Summary

This file establishes foundational linear algebraic structure on the free algebra: it is a **free module** with a **canonical basis indexed by `FreeMonoid X`**, and its **rank equals the cardinality of `List X`**. It also provides a general bound on the rank of algebras generated by a set (`adjoin`). The proofs rely heavily on:
- Equivalence between `FreeAlgebra R X` and `MonoidAlgebra R (FreeMonoid X)`,
- Cardinal arithmetic and universe lifting,
- Properties of `Finsupp`-based bases and module freeness transfer.

This is typical of modern Lean formalization: leveraging library lemmas and structured rewriting to reduce complex algebraic reasoning to known facts.

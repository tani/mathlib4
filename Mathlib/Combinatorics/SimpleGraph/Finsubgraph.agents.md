#### **1. Key Definitions & Theorems**

| Name | Type / Signature | Purpose |

#### **2. Naming Conventions**

- **Prefixes / Suffixes**:
  - `finsubgraph*`: Pertains to finite subgraphs (e.g., `Finsubgraph`, `finsubgraphOfAdj`, `finsubgraphHomFunctor`).
  - `singleton*`: Vertex-generated subgraphs (`singletonFinsubgraph`).
  - `coe*`: Lemmas about coercion from `Finsubgraph` to `Subgraph`.

  - `le_*`: Lemmas about ordering between subgraphs (e.g., `singletonFinsubgraph_le_adj_left`).

#### **4. Proof Logic**

The main proof proceeds as follows:

1. **Reduction to finite codomain**: Use `nonempty_fintype W` to get a `Fintype` on `W`.
2. **Construct inverse system**: Define `finsubgraphHomFunctor` as a functor from `G.Finsubgraphᵒᵖ` to `Type`, where:
   - Objects: Homomorphisms from finite subgraphs to `F`.
   - Morphisms: Restriction along inclusions.
3. **Verify finiteness & nonemptiness**:
   - `Nonempty` of each hom-set follows from the hypothesis `∀ G', G'.verts.Finite → G'.coe →g F`.
   - `Fintype` on each hom-set follows from finiteness of domain and codomain.
4. **Apply compactness**: Use `nonempty_sections_of_finite_inverse_system` to get a compatible family of homomorphisms (a section).
5. **Define global homomorphism**:
   - Vertex map: Use section value at `singletonFinsubgraph v`.
   - Adjacency preservation: Use compatibility of the section with morphisms induced by inclusions of edge-generated subgraphs into vertex-generated ones.

The logical flow is **constructive via compactness**, leveraging categorical limits and inverse systems.

#### 2. **Naming Conventions**

- **Prefixes**:
  - `pretopology_`: for constructions at the level of pretopologies.
  - `grothendieckTopology_`: for constructions at the level of Grothendieck topologies.
  - `isJointlySurjectivePreserving`: typeclass for properties preserved under jointly surjective families.
  - `changeProp`: used to upgrade a `P`-cover to a `Q`-cover when `P ≤ Q`.

- **Suffixes**:
  - `_le_`: for inequalities between (pre)topologies.
  - `_eq_`: for equality statements (e.g., `grothendieckTopology_eq_inf`).
  - `_cover`: for lemmas about covers in the respective topology.

- **Variable naming**:
  - `𝒰`, `𝒱`: cover families (type `Cover`).
  - `S`, `T`: presieves/sieves.
  - `f`, `g`, `h`: morphisms.
  - `x`, `y`, `z`: points (scheme elements).

#### 3. **Tactic Stack**

#### 4. **Proof Logic**

- **Structure**:
  - Proofs are largely **constructive**, leveraging the existence of covers and their properties.
  - **Induction** is not used; instead, proofs rely on:
    - **Choice** (`choose`) to select witnesses from existential hypotheses.
    - **Explicit construction** of new covers (e.g., `U.pullbackCover'`, `U.bind`, `𝒰.changeProp`).
    - **Universal properties** (e.g., pullbacks, bind of presieves).
  - **Equality proofs** (e.g., `grothendieckTopology_eq_inf`) use `le_antisymm`, with each direction proven via:
    - `Pretopology.gi` (Galois insertion) for monotonicity.
    - Manual construction of covers and verification of covering conditions.

- **Common pattern**:
  - To show `T ≤ S`, assume `⟨𝒰, rfl⟩ ∈ S`, construct `𝒰'` such that `𝒰' ∈ T`, and verify `𝒰'` satisfies required properties.

#### Summary

This file formalizes a foundational result in descent theory for schemes: given a multiplicative, base-change-stable morphism property `P`, one can define a (pre)topology on `Scheme` where covers are `P`-families. The main theorem shows this topology coincides with the intersection of the jointly surjective topology and the `P`-topology at the level of Grothendieck topologies. The formalization is highly structured, leveraging typeclasses for properties and explicit constructions for covers.

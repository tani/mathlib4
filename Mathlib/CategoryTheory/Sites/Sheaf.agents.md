## 🧠 **4. Proof Logic & Strategy**

- **Reduction to representables**: Most proofs reduce the sheaf condition in `A` to sheaf conditions in `Type` via the Yoneda embedding (`coyoneda`).  
  - E.g., `isSheaf_iff_isSheafFor`, `isSheaf_iff_isSheaf_of_type`.
- **Limit/colimit characterizations**: Sheaf condition is often expressed as a limit condition over a sieve diagram (`P.mapCone S.arrows.cocone.op`).  
  - `isSheaf_iff_isLimit`, `isSheaf_iff_multifork`, `isSheaf_iff_multiequalizer`.
- **Amalgamation & separation**: Core logic for sheaves is:
  - *Existence*: Compatible local sections glue (`amalgamate`).
  - *Uniqueness*: Glued section is unique (`hom_ext`).
- **Equivalence via universal properties**: Proofs often use:
  - `Cone.isLimitEquivIsTerminal`
  - `IsLimit.postcomposeHomEquiv`
  - `coyonedaJointlyReflectsLimits` (to lift limits through Yoneda).
- **Universe management**: When limits in `A` over diagrams indexed by `C` are needed, a universe-adjusted category `A'` is used (`A' : Type u₂ [Category.{max v₁ u₁} A']`).  
  - See `isSheaf_iff_isSheaf'`, `isSheafForIsSheafFor'`.

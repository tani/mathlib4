| `regularTopology` | `C : Type u → [Category C] → [Preregular C] → Site.CoveredSite C` | Defines the *regular topology* on a preregular category `C`, i.e., the Grothendieck topology generated by effective epimorphic families. |

#### 2. **Naming Conventions**

- **Prefixes**:
  - `mem_sieves_...`: predicates membership of a sieve in a topology.
  - `effectiveEpi_...`: properties or constructions involving effective epimorphisms.
  - `regularTopology_...`: functions/instances related to the regular topology.
- **Suffixes**:
  - `_iff_hasEffectiveEpi`: indicates an equivalence with existence of an effective epi.
  - `_of_hasEffectiveEpi`: one-directional implication (sufficient condition).
- **Other patterns**:
  - `Presieve.ofArrows`: used to construct presieves from arrows.
  - `Sieve.generate`: used to generate a sieve from a presieve.
  - `Saturate.of`, `Saturate.transitive`, etc.: constructors for saturated coverage.

#### 4. **Proof Logic**

- **Structure of `mem_sieves_iff_hasEffectiveEpi`**:
  - **Forward direction** (`→`):  
    Uses *induction on the coverage axioms* (`of`, `top`, `transitive`) to show that any covering sieve must contain an effective epi:
    - `of`: uses the generating condition of the regular coverage (contains a family with effective epi).
    - `top`: uses identity map (which is effective epi).
    - `transitive`: composes effective epis (via instance `EffectiveEpi.comp`) and uses stability.
  - **Backward direction** (`←`):  
    Uses `mem_sieves_of_hasEffectiveEpi`, which lifts an effective epi in the sieve to a covering sieve via saturation of the regular coverage.

- **Key logical flow**:
  - Leverage the definition of `regularTopology` as the *saturated coverage generated by effective epimorphic families*.
  - Use properties of effective epimorphisms (stability under pullback and composition in preregular categories).
  - Apply saturation lemmas (`Coverage.saturate_of_superset`, `Coverage.Saturate.transitive`, etc.).

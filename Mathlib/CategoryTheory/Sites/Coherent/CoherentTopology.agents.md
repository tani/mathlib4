### **Technical Brief: Covering Sieves in the Coherent Topology**

| Name | Type / Signature | Purpose |

| `coherentTopology` | `C → Sieve X → Prop` (via `coherentCoverage C`) | Defines the coherent Grothendieck topology on a precoherent category `C`. |

#### **4. Proof Logic**

  - **→ direction**: Induction on the definition of `coherentTopology` (as the Grothendieck topology generated by `coherentCoverage`):
    - `of`: Extract the effective epi family from the coverage generator.
    - `top`: Trivial case using unit type.
    - `transitive`: Use `EffectiveEpiFamily.transitive_of_finite` to combine families over a covering family.
  - **← direction**: Immediate from `mem_sieves_of_hasEffectiveEpiFamily`.

- **Transitivity proof (`EffectiveEpiFamily.transitive_of_finite`)**:
  - Reduces to showing a certain sieve is in the coherent topology.
  - Uses sheaf condition for the Yoneda embedding (`Sieve.forallYonedaIsSheaf_iff_colimit`).
  - Applies saturation properties of coverages (`Coverage.Saturate.transitive`, `pullback_stable'`).
  - Relies on finite indexing to construct the composite family.

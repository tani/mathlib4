| Name | Type / Purpose |
|------|----------------|
| `extensiveTopology.mem_sieves_iff_contains_colimit_cofan` | **Main theorem**: Characterizes covering sieves for the *extensive topology* on a category `C`. A sieve `S` on `X` is a covering sieve iff it contains a finite family of arrows `π a : Y a ⟶ X` such that `X` is the colimit (coproduct) of the `Y a`, and each `π a` is in `S`. |
| `extensiveTopology` | The Grothendieck topology on `C` generated by the *extensive coverage*, where covering families are finite families of morphisms with common codomain that jointly exhibit the codomain as a coproduct of their domains. |
| `extensiveCoverage` | The coverage defining the extensive topology: a sieve `S` on `X` is in `extensiveCoverage X` iff it contains a finite family of arrows `π a : Y a ⟶ X` such that `X` is the coproduct of the `Y a` with coproduct injections `π a`. |
| `IsColimit (Cofan.mk X π)` | States that the cone given by `X` and the family `π` is a colimit cocone — i.e., `X` is the coproduct of the `Y a`. |
| `Presieve.ofArrows Y π` | The presieve generated by the family `π`, i.e., `λ f, ∃ a, f = π a ≫ f'` (used to relate sieves to families of arrows). |

- **Key logical flow**:

### **4. Proof Logic & Strategy**

- **Gluing via sheaf condition**: Most constructions (e.g., `appHom`, `sheafHom`) rely on:
  - Cover-dense ⇒ existence of a covering sieve factoring through `G`
  - Locally-full ⇒ control over morphisms in image of `G`
  - Sheaf condition ⇒ unique amalgamation of compatible families

- **Uniqueness via `ext` lemmas**:
  - `Functor.IsCoverDense.ext`: equality of sheaf sections determined by behavior on `G`-images
  - `IsLocallyFull.ext`, `IsLocallyFaithful.ext`: equality of morphisms determined on `G`-images

- **Equivalence arguments**:
  - `restrictHomEquivHom`: shows `whiskerLeft G.op` is an equivalence of hom-sets
  - `sheafHom_restrict_eq`, `sheafHom_eq`: inverse laws

- **Iso detection**:
  - `iso_of_restrict_iso`: uses `sheafIso` + `presheafIso` to lift isos from restriction

- **Dense subsite logic**:
  - `IsDenseSubsite` combines:
    - Cover-dense + locally fully faithful (for comparison lemma)
    - Cover correspondence (`S ∈ J ⇔ G_* S ∈ K`) for site equivalence

### Technical Metadata Brief

#### 1. **Key Definitions & Theorems**

| Name | Type / Signature | Purpose |
|------|------------------|---------|
| `pretopology` | `P : MorphismProperty C → [P.IsMultiplicative] → [P.IsStableUnderBaseChange] → Pretopology C` | Constructs a pretopology on `C` where a sieve `S` at `X` is a covering iff all its elements satisfy `P`. |
| `grothendieckTopology` | `P → GrothendieckTopology C` | Associates the Grothendieck topology generated by `P.pretopology`. |
| `pretopology_le` | `P ≤ Q → P.pretopology ≤ Q.pretopology` | Monotonicity of the construction: if `P ⊆ Q`, then the induced pretopology for `P` is coarser than for `Q`. |
| `pretopology_inf` | `(P ⊓ Q).pretopology = P.pretopology ⊓ Q.pretopology` | The construction preserves binary infima (meets) in the lattice of morphism properties. |

#### 2. **Naming Conventions**

- **Prefixes / Suffixes**:
  - `pretopology`: suffix used for the induced pretopology construction.
  - `grothendieckTopology`: suffix for the associated Grothendieck topology.
  - `IsMultiplicative`, `IsStableUnderBaseChange`: typeclass interfaces encoding structural properties of `P`.
  - `le`, `inf`: standard lattice-theoretic names for order and meet operations.

#### 3. **Tactic Stack**

- **Core tactics used**:
  - `cases`: for destructuring existential hypotheses (`hg`, `hf`).
  - `exact`: to apply typeclass instances or lemmas directly.
  - `apply`: to apply lemmas like `P.of_isIso`, `P.pullback_snd`, `comp_mem`.
  - `obtain ⟨...⟩ := ...`: for destructuring existential statements (e.g., pullback data).
  - `ext`: extensionality for proving equality of sieves / pretopologies.
  - `aesop`, `simp_rw`, `ring`: *not present* in this snippet — proof is mostly manual and structural.

#### 4. **Proof Logic**

- **Structure**:
  - **Definition of `pretopology`**: Proves the four axioms of a pretopology:
    1. **Identities**: `has_isos` uses `P.of_isIso`.
    2. **Stability under pullback**: `pullbacks` uses `P.pullback_snd`.
    3. **Transitivity**: `transitive` uses `comp_mem` and hypothesis `hTi`.
  - **Monotonicity (`pretopology_le`)**: Directly follows from pointwise inclusion `P ≤ Q`.
  - **Infimum (`pretopology_inf`)**: Uses extensionality and splits the sieve condition into components.

- **Logical Flow**:
  - Most proofs are *constructive* and *element-wise*, leveraging the definition of sieves and the assumed properties of `P`.
  - Pullback and transitivity proofs rely on *factorization* of morphisms and *sieve composition*.

#### 5. **Imports**

- `Mathlib.CategoryTheory.MorphismProperty.Limits`: Provides `MorphismProperty`, `IsMultiplicative`, `IsStableUnderBaseChange`, and related lemmas (e.g., `pullback_snd`, `comp_mem`, `of_isIso`).
- `Mathlib.CategoryTheory.Sites.Pretopology`: Defines `Pretopology`, `GrothendieckTopology`, and their operations (`toGrothendieck`, `≤`, `⊔`, `⊓`).

---

This module formalizes a foundational result in categorical topology: *multiplicative, pullback-stable morphism properties induce pretopologies*, a key step toward constructing sites like the étale site in algebraic geometry.

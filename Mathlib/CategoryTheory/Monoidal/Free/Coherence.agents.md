#### **4. Proof Logic**

- **High-level strategy**:
  1. Define *normal forms* for objects (left-associated tensors with unit on the left).
  2. Construct a *normalization functor* `fullNormalize : F C → N C`.
  3. Show that every object `X` is isomorphic to its normal form via `fullNormalizeIso`.
  4. Prove naturality of this isomorphism (`normalize_naturality`), implying that any two parallel morphisms factor through a discrete category (hence equal).
  5. Conclude thinness (`subsingleton_hom`) and invertibility (`Groupoid` instance).

- **Key logical insight**:
  > If two morphisms `f, g : X → Y` exist, then `(fullNormalize f) = (fullNormalize g)` (since `N C` is discrete), and naturality of `fullNormalizeIso` forces `f = g`.

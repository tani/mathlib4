#### **4. Proof Logic**

- **General Strategy**:
  - **Concrete → Abstract**: Define explicit models (`limitCone`, `colimitCocone`) and prove they satisfy universal properties (`limitConeIsLimit`, `colimitCoconeIsColimit`).
  - **Abstract ↔ Concrete**: Use equivalences (`isLimitEquivSections`, `colimitEquivQuot`) to relate abstract limits/colimits to concrete constructions.
  - **Universe Handling**: Two parallel constructions (`Small` vs `TypeMax`) to manage universe levels; `Small` uses `Shrink` to ensure smallness, `TypeMax` avoids it.
  - **Section-based Characterization**: Limits in `Type` are characterized via sections of `F`, i.e., natural transformations `const J X ⟶ F`.
  - **Quotient-based Colimits**: Colimits are quotients of `Σ j, F.obj j` by the equivalence generated by morphisms in `J`.

- **Common Patterns**:
  - Prove `IsLimit c ↔ ∀ s ∈ F.sections, ∃! x, ...` → then construct equivalence.
  - Use `Quot.induction` or `Quot.sound` to reason about colimit elements.
  - Use `limit_ext`/`colimit_ext` for uniqueness of morphisms out of limits/in to colimits.
  - Use `coyoneda`/`yoneda` embeddings to relate cones/cocones to limits of hom-sets.

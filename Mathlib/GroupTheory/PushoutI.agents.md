### Technical Metadata Brief: `Mathlib.GroupTheory.Pushout`

---

#### **1. Key Definitions & Theorems**

| Name | Type / Signature | Purpose |
|------|------------------|---------|
| `PushoutI.con` | `Con (Coprod (CoprodI G) H)` | Congruence relation used to define the pushout as a quotient. Generated by identifying `φ i x` in `G i` with `x` in `H`. |
| `PushoutI` | `Type _` | The pushout (amalgamated product) of a diagram of monoids/groups indexed by `ι`. |
| `of i : G i →* PushoutI φ` | Monoid homomorphism | Canonical map from each `G i` into the pushout. |
| `base : H →* PushoutI φ` | Monoid homomorphism | Canonical map from the base monoid `H` into the pushout. |
| `lift f k hf` | `PushoutI φ →* K` | Universal property: given compatible family `f i : G i →* K` and `k : H →* K`, constructs unique map out of pushout. |
| `hom_ext` / `hom_ext_nonempty` | Extensionality for homs out of pushout | Two homs are equal if they agree on all `of i` and `base`. |
| `homEquiv` | `(PushoutI φ →* K) ≃ ...` | Equivalence expressing universal property as a type equivalence. |
| `NormalWord` | `Type _` | Normal form for elements of the pushout: `head : H × word in coproduct with letters in transversal`. |
| `Transversal` | `Type _` | Choice of coset representatives for `H ↪ G i`, with `1` in each coset set and `IsComplement`. |
| `equiv : PushoutI φ ≃ NormalWord d` | Equivalence | Normal form theorem: every element of the pushout corresponds uniquely to a normal word. |
| `of_injective` | `Injective (of i)` | If all `φ i` are injective, then the canonical maps `of i` into the pushout are injective. |
| `base_injective` | `Injective (base)` | Same as above for `base`. |
| `Reduced` | `Word G → Prop` | A word is *reduced* if no letter lies in the image of `φ`. |
| `Reduced.eq_empty_of_mem_range` | `Reduced φ w → ofCoprodI w.prod ∈ range base → w = []` | A reduced nonempty word cannot lie in the image of `base`. |
| `inf_of_range_eq_base_range` | `(of i).range ⊓ (of j).range = (base φ).range` | Intersection of images of two distinct summands is exactly the image of the base. |

#### **2. Naming Conventions**

- **Prefixes**:
  - `of_`: canonical maps from components (`of i`, `ofCoprodI`)
  - `base_`: maps from the amalgamating monoid (`base`, `base_smul`, `base_injective`)
  - `lift_`: universal property constructions (`lift`, `lift_of`, `lift_base`)
  - `prod_`: product of a normal word (`prod`, `prod_smul`, `prod_injective`)
  - `equiv_`: equivalences (`equiv`, `equivPair`, `equiv_snd_eq_*`)
  - `cons_`, `rcons_`: constructors for normal words/pairs
  - `transversal_`: properties of transversal choices (`transversal_nonempty`, `one_mem`, `compl`)

- **Suffixes**:
  - `_def'`: definitional lemmas for actions (`base_smul_def'`, `summand_smul_def'`)
  - `_eq_smul`: relate categorical actions to group actions (`of_smul_eq_smul`, `base_smul_eq_smul`)
  - `_smul`: actions of groups/monoids on normal words (`base_smul`, `summand_smul`)
  - `_recOn`, `_induction_on`: eliminators/induction principles (`consRecOn`, `induction_on`)
  - `_injective`: injectivity results (`of_injective`, `base_injective`, `prod_injective`)
  - `_range`: membership in image of maps (`eq_empty_of_mem_range`, `inf_of_range_eq_base_range`)

#### **3. Tactic Stack**

- **Core proof automation**:
  - `simp` / `simp_all` — heavily used for simplification of definitions, especially in action lemmas and normal form proofs.
  - `rw` — rewriting using equalities from `of_apply_eq_base`, `of_comp_eq_base`, etc.
  - `ext` — extensionality for homomorphisms and normal words.
  - `induction ... using ...` — especially `consRecOn`, `induction_on`, `Word.consRecOn`, `Coprod.induction_on`, `Con.induction_on`.
  - `cases` / `rcases` — destructuring dependent pairs, words, normal forms.
  - `apply` / `exact` — for applying lemmas and constructing proofs.
  - `have`, `suffices`, `by_contra` — intermediate claims and contradiction arguments (e.g., in `inf_of_range_eq_base_range`).
  - `convert` — for inductive steps where structure matches up to definitional equality.
  - `push_neg` — negation normalization in logical manipulations.
  - `dsimp`, `erw` — definitional simplification and rewriting.

- **Advanced/Domain-specific**:
  - `MonoidHom.cancel_right`, `Function.Injective.of_comp` — for injectivity proofs.
  - `equiv`-based reasoning: `equiv.apply_symm_apply`, `equiv_fst_eq_mul_inv`, etc.
  - `SetLike.mem_coe`, `Subgroup.coe_mul`, `Subgroup.mem_range` — for subgroup/coset reasoning.

#### **4. Proof Logic**

- **Structure of proofs**:
  - **Inductive on syntax**: Many proofs (e.g., `prod_smul_empty`, `consRecOn`, `induction_on`) proceed by induction on words or normal forms using `consRecOn` or `Word.consRecOn`.
  - **Transversal-based normal form**: To prove injectivity or faithfulness, pick a transversal `d` (using `transversal_nonempty`), then use the equivalence `equiv : PushoutI φ ≃ NormalWord d` to reduce statements to actions on normal words.
  - **Faithful action ⇒ injectivity**: Prove that the action of `PushoutI φ` (or `G i`, `H`) on `NormalWord d` is faithful, then deduce injectivity of maps via `Function.Injective.of_comp`.
  - **Reduced words**: Use `Reduced` to isolate words not involving base group elements; prove key lemmas like `eq_empty_of_mem_range` by lifting to normal words and using `prod_smul_empty`.
  - **Intersection argument**: For `inf_of_range_eq_base_range`, use contradiction: assume an element lies in both `of i` and `of j` ranges but not in `base`, construct a reduced word whose product lies in `base`, contradicting `eq_empty_of_mem_range`.

- **Key logical flow**:
  1. Define congruence `con` and quotient `PushoutI`.
  2. Prove universal property (`lift`, `homEquiv`, `hom_ext`).
  3. Assume groups → define group structure on `PushoutI`.
  4. Introduce `Transversal`, `NormalWord`, `Pair`, `equivPair`, `equiv`.
  5. Prove `equiv` is well-defined and bijective (`prod_smul_empty`, `prod_injective`).
  6. Use faithful action to prove `of_injective`, `base_injective`.
  7. Define `Reduced`, prove `eq_empty_of_mem_range`.
  8. Use above to prove structural properties like `inf_of_range_eq_base_range`.

### **Technical Brief: Gluing Topological Spaces in Lean 4**

#### **1. Key Definitions & Theorems**

| Name | Type / Purpose |
|------|----------------|
| `GlueData` | A structure encoding a family of topological spaces `U i`, open embeddings `f i j : V i j ↪ U i`, and transition maps `t i j : V i j → V j i`, satisfying coherence conditions (e.g., cocycle condition). |
| `glued` | The glued space: defined as the multicoequalizer of `∐_{i,j} V i j ⇉ ∐_i U i`. |
| `ι i` | The canonical immersion `U i ⟶ glued`, part of the colimit cocone. |
| `Rel` | A binary relation on `Σ i, U i` defined by `⟨i, x⟩ ~ ⟨j, y⟩` iff `x = y` (same component) or `∃ z ∈ V i j, f i j z = x ∧ f j i (t i j z) = y`. |
| `mk'` | A constructor for `GlueData` using open subsets and elementwise conditions (easier to use than categorical one). |
| `ofOpenSubsets` | Constructs `GlueData` from a family of open subsets `U : J → Opens α`. |
| `fromOpenSubsetsGlue` | The canonical map `glued → α` induced by the inclusions `U i ↪ α`. |
| `openCoverGlueHomeo` | If `⋃ i, U i = ⊤`, then `glued ≃ₜ α`. |

**Key Theorems:**
- `ι_eq_iff_rel`: `ι i x = ι j y ↔ Rel ⟨i, x⟩ ⟨j, y⟩`
- `rel_equiv`: `Rel` is an equivalence relation.
- `isOpen_iff`: A subset of `glued` is open iff all its preimages under `ι i` are open.
- `ι_jointly_surjective`: The `ι i` are jointly surjective.
- `ι_isOpenEmbedding`: Each `ι i` is an open embedding.
- `image_inter`: `range (ι i) ∩ range (ι j) = range (f i j ≫ ι _)`
- `preimage_range`: `ι j ⁻¹' range (ι i) = range (f j i)`
- `preimage_image_eq_image`: Describes preimage of image of a subset under `ι i` via transition maps.
- `fromOpenSubsetsGlue_isOpenEmbedding`: The canonical map from `glued` to `α` is an open embedding.
- `range_fromOpenSubsetsGlue`: Its range is `⋃ i, U i`.
- `openCoverGlueHomeo`: When the family is an open cover, `glued ≃ₜ α`.

  - `f_`: structure maps (open embeddings `V i j ↪ U i`).

#### **4. Proof Logic**

- **Structure of proofs:**
  - Most proofs proceed by unfolding definitions (`delta`, `simp_rw`) and reducing to elementwise reasoning.
  - Key lemmas like `ι_eq_iff_rel` are proven via two directions:
    - `→`: use properties of colimits, coequalizers, and the equivalence relation generated by the diagram.
    - `←`: use the universal property of the colimit and the defining equations (`glue_condition_apply`).
  - Set-theoretic properties (`preimage_image_eq_image`, `image_inter`) are proven by extensionality (`ext x`) and case analysis on `Rel`.
  - Continuity/openness proofs often use `isOpen_iff` and `isOpenMap` lemmas for open embeddings.
  - The cocycle condition is verified using `t_fac_assoc`, `cocycle_assoc`, and properties of `t'`.

- **Induction/Coherence:**
  - No explicit induction; coherence conditions (e.g., `t' i j k ≫ t' j k i ≫ t' k i j = 𝟙`) are handled via categorical identities and `Iso` properties.

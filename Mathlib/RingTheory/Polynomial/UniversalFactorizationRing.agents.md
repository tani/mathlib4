### **4. PROOF LOGIC**

- **Representability arguments**:
  - Use `mapEquivMonic` to translate between algebra maps and monic polynomials.
  - Construct representing objects via tensor products and base change.

- **Universal properties**:
  - Prove `homEquiv` by constructing mutually inverse maps using `aeval`, `TensorProduct.lift`, and `mapEquivMonic.symm`.

- **Finite presentation**:
  - Build `universalFactorizationMapPresentation` as a *pre-submersive presentation*.
  - Show it satisfies the conditions: kernel generated by relations, Jacobian matrix computed via Sylvester matrix.

- **Resultant & Jacobian**:
  - Compute Jacobian matrix via `pderiv` on `universalFactorizationMap`.
  - Use properties of Sylvester matrix and resultant to relate to Jacobian determinant.

- **Coprime factorization**:
  - Localize at the Jacobian (resultant) to invert it ⇒ ensures coprimeness.
  - Prove étaleness via submersive presentation + invertibility of Jacobian.

- **Inductive arguments**:
  - Prove integrality/finiteness by induction on `MvPolynomial` generators (`X i`, `C a`, sums, products).
  - Use `isIntegral_coeff_of_dvd` for integrality of coefficients.

#### **File Overview**
- Constructs *universal objects* for:
  1. Monic polynomials of fixed degree (`MvPolynomial (Fin n) R`).
  2. Factorizations into fixed-degree monic factors (`UniversalFactorizationRing`).
  3. Coprime factorizations (`UniversalCoprimeFactorizationRing`, étale over `R`).
- Uses:
  - `MvPolynomial` as coordinate rings for universal monic polynomials.
  - Tensor products to encode multiplication of polynomials.
  - Resultants to detect coprimeness.
  - Localization to enforce invertibility of the resultant.
- Proves:
  - Representability via `homEquiv`.
  - Finite presentation & integrality.
  - Étaleness of the coprime factorization ring.

### **Technical Brief: `IsAdjoinRoot` and `IsAdjoinRootMonic`**

#### **1. Key Definitions & Theorems**

| Name | Type | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| `IsAdjoinRoot S f` | `Type (max u v)` | Predicate stating that `S` is generated by adjoining a root of `f : R[X]` to `R`. Bundles: <br> • `map : R[X] →+* S` <br> • `map_surjective` <br> • `ker_map = Ideal.span {f}` <br> • `algebraMap_eq` |
| `IsAdjoinRootMonic S f` | `Type (max u v)` | Extension of `IsAdjoinRoot` for monic `f`. Adds `Monic f`. Enables unique polynomial representatives via `modByMonicHom`. |
| `root h` | `S` | The image of `X` under `h.map`, i.e., the adjoined root. |
| `repr h x` | `R[X]` | Arbitrary preimage of `x : S` under `h.map`. Non-unique unless `f` is monic. |
| `lift h hx` | `S →+* T` | Lifts a ring homomorphism `R →+* T` to `S →+* T` using a root `x ∈ T` of `f`. |
| `liftHom h x hx'` | `S →ₐ[R] T` | Algebraic lift (commutes with `algebraMap R T`). |
| `modByMonicHom h` | `S →ₗ[R] R[X]` | For monic `f`, gives *unique* representative modulo `f`. |
| `basis h` | `Basis (Fin (natDegree f)) R S` | Power basis generated by `root h`. |
| `powerBasis h` | `PowerBasis R S` | Bundles `root h` as generator of a power basis. |
| `aequiv h h'` | `S ≃ₐ[R] T` | Canonical algebra isomorphism between two realizations of adjoining the same root. |
| `ofEquiv h e` | `IsAdjoinRoot T f` | Transfers `IsAdjoinRoot` structure along an algebra isomorphism `e : S ≃ₐ[R] T`. |
| `minpoly_eq h hirr` | `minpoly R (root h) = f` | If `f` is monic, irreducible, and `R` is a GCD domain + integrally closed, then `f` is the minimal polynomial of the adjoined root. |

### Technical Brief: Discriminant of a Family of Vectors in `Algebra`

---

#### **1. Key Definitions & Theorems**

| Name | Type / Signature | Purpose |
|------|------------------|---------|
| `discr` | `discr (A : Type u) {B : Type v} [CommRing A] [CommRing B] [Algebra A B] [Fintype ι] (b : ι → B) := (traceMatrix A b).det` | Defines the discriminant of a family `b : ι → B` as the determinant of the trace matrix `(trace A B (b i * b j))_{i,j}`. |
| `discr_zero_of_not_linearIndependent` | `{b : ι → B} → ¬LinearIndependent A b → discr A b = 0` | Shows discriminant vanishes if the family is not linearly independent (over a domain `A`). |
| `discr_of_matrix_vecMul` / `discr_of_matrix_mulVec` | `(b : ι → B) → (P : Matrix ι ι A) → discr A (b ᵥ* P.map _) = P.det² * discr A b`<br>`discr A (P.map _ *ᵥ b) = P.det² * discr A b` | Describes how discriminant transforms under change of basis via matrix multiplication (left/right). |
| `discr_not_zero_of_basis` | `[IsSeparable K L] → (b : Basis ι K L) → discr K b ≠ 0` | Non-vanishing of discriminant for a basis in a finite separable field extension. |
| `discr_isUnit_of_basis` | `[IsSeparable K L] → (b : Basis ι K L) → IsUnit (discr K b)` | Immediate corollary: discriminant is invertible in `K`. |
| `discr_eq_det_embeddingsMatrixReindex_pow_two` | `[IsSeparable K L] → (e : ι ≃ (L →ₐ[K] E)) → algebraMap K E (discr K b) = (embeddingsMatrixReindex K E b e).det²` | Relates discriminant to square of determinant of embedding matrix into an algebraically closed field `E`. |
| `discr_powerBasis_eq_prod` | `[IsSeparable K L] → (e : Fin pb.dim ≃ (L →ₐ[K] E)) → algebraMap K E (discr K pb.basis) = ∏_{i < j} (σ_j(gen) - σ_i(gen))²` | Explicit product formula for discriminant of a power basis. |
| `discr_powerBasis_eq_norm` | `[IsSeparable K L] → discr K pb.basis = (-1)^{n(n-1)/2} * norm K (minpoly pb.gen.derivative)` | Discriminant expressed via field norm and derivative of minimal polynomial. |
| `discr_isIntegral` | `(∀ i, IsIntegral R (b i)) → IsIntegral R (discr K b)` | Integrality of discriminant when all `b i` are integral over `R`. |
| `discr_mul_isIntegral_mem_adjoin` | `[IsSeparable K L] [IsIntegrallyClosed R] [IsFractionRing R K] → (z : L) → IsIntegral R z → discr K B.basis • z ∈ adjoin R ({B.gen})` | Key arithmetic application: discriminant times integral element lies in the subring generated by the power basis generator. |
| `discr_eq_discr` | `(b b' : Basis ι ℤ A) → discr ℤ b = discr ℤ b'` | Discriminant is independent of choice of ℤ-basis (for free ℤ-modules). |

#### **2. Naming Conventions**

- **Prefixes**:
  - `discr_`: All discriminant-related definitions and theorems start with this.
  - `isIntegral_`, `isUnit_`, `isSeparable_`: Properties of elements/modules/extensions.
  - `mem_`, `subset_`, `sum_`, `prod_`: Set/structure membership and operations.
- **Suffixes**:
  - `_of_`: Conditions or inputs (e.g., `discr_zero_of_not_linearIndependent`).
  - `_eq_`: Equality statements (e.g., `discr_eq_discr`, `discr_powerBasis_eq_prod`).
  - `_mem_`: Membership results (e.g., `discr_mul_isIntegral_mem_adjoin`).
- **Matrix/Vector ops**:
  - `vecMul`, `mulVec`: Right/left multiplication of vectors by matrices.
  - `traceMatrix`, `embeddingsMatrixReindex`, `vandermonde`: Matrix constructions.

#### **4. Proof Logic**

- **Structure of proofs**:
  - **Definitional reductions**: Most proofs begin by unfolding `discr_def` (`discr = det traceMatrix`).
  - **Matrix identities**: Use of `traceMatrix_of_basis`, `traceMatrix_of_matrix_vecMul`, `det_mul`, `det_transpose`.
  - **Linear algebra over fields**: For separable extensions, rely on nondegeneracy of trace form (`traceForm_nondegenerate`) and properties of embeddings.
  - **Product manipulations**: For power basis, reduce to Vandermonde determinant via `embeddingsMatrixReindex_eq_vandermonde`, then apply `det_vandermonde`.
  - **Integral closure arguments**: Use `IsIntegral.det`, `isIntegral_trace`, and `IsIntegrallyClosed.isIntegral_iff`.
  - **Cramer’s rule**: In `discr_mul_isIntegral_mem_adjoin`, apply Cramer’s rule to express `z` in terms of trace dual basis, then use integrality and integrally closed hypothesis.

- **Common patterns**:
  - Induction is *not* used — proofs rely on structural properties (e.g., basis change, separability, integrality).
  - Equivalence of finite types (`ι ≃ Fin n`) is used to reindex products and matrices.
  - `algebraMap K E` injectivity is used to lift equalities from `E` back to `K`.

**Scope**:  
- General setting: `A`-algebra `B`, family `b : ι → B`.  
- Field case: `K ⊆ L` finite separable extension, `E` algebraically closed over `K`.  
- Arithmetic case: `R` integrally closed domain, `K = Frac(R)`, `L/K` finite separable, `B` power basis with integral generator.  
- Integral case: `R → K → L` scalar tower, integrality of elements and discriminant.

### Summary

This file formalizes the discriminant in full generality for algebra families, with deep connections to linear algebra, field theory, and arithmetic. It culminates in powerful arithmetic applications (e.g., integrality and ideal membership), leveraging tools like trace forms, embeddings, norms, and Cramer’s rule. The naming and structure follow Lean/Mathlib conventions precisely, with heavy reliance on matrix identities and algebraic properties rather than inductive arguments.

- **Inductive structure**: Most proofs about filtrations proceed by induction on `i`, `n`, or `k`, especially for lemmas like `pow_smul_le`, `stable_iff_exists_pow_smul_eq_of_ge`.
- **Reduction to stable case**: Artin–Rees and Krull’s theorems are proven via reduction to stable filtrations (`stableFiltration ⊓ trivialFiltration`).

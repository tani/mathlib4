### 4. PROOF LOGIC

The logical flow follows a **minimal-relation strategy**:

1. **Setup**: Assume characteristic `p > 0`, and that `p`-th powers preserve linear independence (`H`).
2. **Construct minimal relation**: Let `F` be a nonzero polynomial of minimal total degree vanishing at `a`.
3. **Irreducibility**: Show `F` is irreducible via minimality (`irreducible_of_forall_totalDegree_le`).
4. **Univariate restriction**: Define `toPolynomialAdjoinImageCompl F a i`, and show:
   - It vanishes at `a i` (`aeval_toPolynomialAdjoinImageCompl_eq_zero`)
   - Its coefficients remain nonzero (`coeff_toPolynomialAdjoinImageCompl_ne_zero`)
   - It remains irreducible under algebraic independence (`irreducible_toPolynomialAdjoinImageCompl`)
5. **Exponent non-divisibility**: Use `H` to find a monomial in `F` whose exponent vector has some component not divisible by `p`.
6. **Algebraicity & separability**:
   - From `i ∈ F.vars`, deduce `a i` is algebraic over `adjoin k (a '' {i}ᶜ)`
   - Use minimal polynomial separability criteria (via `minpoly.separable_or`) and contradiction to deduce separability.
7. **Basis selection**:
   - For transcendence basis missing one element, find one element to remove to get a *separable* transcendence basis.
   - Extend to finite generation via maximal separable closure argument.

### 7. SUMMARY

This file formalizes a key direction of the equivalence between separable generation and linear independence of `p`-th powers in positive characteristic. It leverages minimal-degree relations in multivariate polynomial rings, irreducibility, and separability criteria via minimal polynomials. The structure is highly modular, with reusable lemmas for later equivalence proofs (e.g., (1) ⇔ (2) ⇔ (3) ⇔ (4) ⇔ (5)). The final theorem confirms that over perfect fields, all finitely generated extensions are separably generated — a foundational result in algebraic geometry and field theory.

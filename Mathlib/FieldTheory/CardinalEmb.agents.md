### **4. Proof Logic**

- **High-level strategy**:
  1. **Filtration construction**: Use transfinite recursion (`wellFounded_lt.fix`) to build a strictly increasing chain of intermediate fields `E⟮<i⟯` indexed by `ι = rank(F E).ord.toType`.
  2. **Successor step**: At each successor `i⁺`, the number of extensions of an embedding `E⟮<i⟯ → Ē` is `#(X i)`, finite and ≥ 2 (if separable).
  3. **Limit step**: Use inverse limit (`equivLim`) to glue embeddings over a limit ordinal.
  4. **Global bijection**: Combine all steps via `embEquivPi`, reducing `Field.Emb F E` to a product over `ι`.
  5. **Cardinal arithmetic**: Use `mk_pi`, `prod_le_prod`, and properties of infinite cardinals to conclude `#Emb = 2^rank` or `2^sepDegree`.

- **Inductive / Recursive structure**:
  - Transfinite induction on `ι` using `wellFounded_lt.fix`.
  - Use of `SuccOrder.prelimitRecOn` (implicit in `equivSucc`, `equivLim`) to define global maps from local data.
  - `WithTop ι` to handle top element (the whole field `E`) uniformly.

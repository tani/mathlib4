  - `map_...`, `comap_...`: for behavior under homomorphisms.

#### **4. Proof Logic**

- **Structure of proofs**:
  - **Membership characterizations**: Often reduce to `IsIntegral` or `IsAlgebraic`, then apply `isAlgebraic_iff_isIntegral`.
  - **Homomorphism compatibility**: Use `map_mem_algebraicClosure_iff` and `comap_eq_of_algHom` to reduce to element-wise reasoning.
  - **Equality of subfields**: Prove double inclusion (`le_antisymm`) or use `equivOfEq` on `toSubalgebra` equalities.
  - **Induction/Transitivity**: For tower arguments (`tower_top`, `isIntegral_trans`), often rely on `IsScalarTower` and `Algebra.ofId`.
  - **Normality/Algebraicity**: Use `normalClosure_le_iff`, `le_algebraicClosure_iff`, and `isAlgebraic_iff`.

- **Common patterns**:
  - `obtain ⟨x, rfl⟩ := ...` for eliminating existential quantifiers.
  - `have h : ..., from ...` to introduce intermediate facts.
  - `simp only [mem_algebraicClosure_iff, ...]` to simplify membership goals.

### Summary

This file formalizes the **relative algebraic closure** in field extensions, establishing its foundational properties: characterization by algebraicity/integrality, behavior under homomorphisms and isomorphisms, interaction with towers, normal closures, and algebraic closures. It leverages Mathlib’s rich infrastructure for intermediate fields, integral closures, and algebraic extensions, with proofs relying heavily on simplification, extensionality, and transitivity arguments.

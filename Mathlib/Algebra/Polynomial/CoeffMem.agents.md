### Technical Brief: Coefficient Bounds for Polynomial Division with Monic Divisors

---

#### **1. Key Definitions & Theorems**

| Name | Type / Signature | Purpose |
|------|------------------|---------|
| `divModByMonicAux` | `p q : S[X] → q.Monic → ℕ → S × S` | Recursive auxiliary function computing quotient and remainder in polynomial division by a monic polynomial. |
| `coeff_divModByMonicAux_mem_span_pow_mul_span` | `∀ p q, q.Monic → ∀ i, (divModByMonicAux ...).1.coeff i ∈ spanCoeffs(q) ^ deg(p) * spanCoeffs(p) ∧ ...` | Core lemma bounding coefficients of intermediate quotient/remainder steps: each coefficient lies in the product of `spanCoeffs(q)` raised to `deg(p)` and `spanCoeffs(p)`. Proved by induction on `deg(p)`. |
| `coeff_modByMonic_mem_pow_natDegree_mul` | `p q : S[X] → Mp, Mq : Submodule R S → hp, hp', hq, hq' → ∀ i, (p %ₘ q).coeff i ∈ Mq ^ p.natDegree * Mp` | Bounds coefficients of the *remainder* `p %ₘ q` in terms of submodules containing coefficients of `p` and `q`. Each coefficient is a sum of products with ≤1 coefficient from `p` and `deg(p)` from `q`. |
| `coeff_divByMonic_mem_pow_natDegree_mul` | Same premises as above, conclusion: `(p /ₘ q).coeff i ∈ Mq ^ p.natDegree * Mp` | Bounds coefficients of the *quotient* `p /ₘ q` analogously. |
| `idealSpan_range_update_divByMonic` | `i ≠ j → v : ι → R[X] → (v i).Monic ⇒ span(range(update v j (v j %ₘ v i))) = span(range v)` | Shows that replacing `v j` by its remainder modulo monic `v i` does not change the ideal/span generated by the family `v`. Used in Gröbner-style or Buchberger-like arguments. |

**Notation & Shorthands Used**:
- `deg(p) := natDegree p`
- `coeffs(p) := Set.range (coeff p)`
- `spanCoeffs(p) := 1 ⊔ Submodule.span R coeffs(p)` (i.e., the submodule generated by coefficients of `p`, extended by `1`)
- `p %ₘ q`, `p /ₘ q`: monic polynomial division remainder and quotient (defined via `divModByMonicAux`)

---

#### **2. Naming Conventions**

- **Prefixes**:
  - `coeff_...`: Lemmas about individual coefficients.
  - `idealSpan_...`: Lemmas about spans/ideals generated by sets of polynomials.
- **Suffixes**:
  - `_mem_span_pow_mul`: Indicates membership in a product of a power of a span and another span.
  - `_divByMonic`, `_modByMonic`: Pertains to quotient/remainder operations w.r.t. monic divisors.
  - `_update_...`: Pertains to updating a family of polynomials at one index.
- **Helper abbreviations**:
  - `Mp`, `Mq`: Submodules bounding coefficients of `p`, `q`.
  - `hp`, `hq`: Hypotheses that coefficients lie in respective submodules.
  - `hp'`, `hq'`: Hypotheses that `1 ∈ Mp`, `1 ∈ Mq` (needed for unital closure in submodule products).

---

#### **3. Tactic Stack**

The proofs rely heavily on:
- `rw` / `simp` / `simp_rw`: Rewriting definitions (`divModByMonicAux`, `modByMonic`, `divByMonic`, `span`, etc.)
- `split_ifs`: Handling `if-then-else` branches in recursive definitions.
- `gcongr`: For monotonicity goals involving submodule inclusions (e.g., `≤`, `≤'`).
- `exacts [...]`: Supplying multiple goals in sequence.
- `calc`: Chain reasoning for inequalities (especially in bounding powers).
- `have H := calc ...`: Intermediate lemmas for degree and submodule containment.
- `termination_by p => deg(p)`: Well-founded induction on natural degree.

Key automation:
- `aesop` is *not* used — proofs are largely manual and structural.
- `ring` / `abel` are *not* used — arithmetic is handled via `simp` and `rw`.

---

#### **4. Proof Logic**

- **Inductive structure** on `deg(p)` for `coeff_divModByMonicAux_mem_span_pow_mul_span`:
  1. Base case: `p = 0` or `deg(p) < deg(q)` → quotient is 0, remainder is `p`.
  2. Inductive step: Reduce to `r = p - q * (lc(p) * X^{deg(p)-deg(q)})`, which has strictly smaller degree.
  3. Use induction hypothesis on `r`, then combine bounds using:
     - Submodule properties (`sub_mem`, `add_mem`, `mul_mem_mul`, `pow_succ`)
     - Degree inequality `deg(r) < deg(p)`
     - Inclusion `spanCoeffs(r) ≤ spanCoeffs(p) ⊔ spanCoeffs(q) * spanCoeffs(p)`
- **For `coeff_modByMonic_mem_pow_natDegree_mul` and `coeff_divByMonic_mem_pow_natDegree_mul`**:
  - Reduce to `divModByMonicAux` via definitions.
  - Apply the main auxiliary lemma.
  - Use `gcongr` to lift submodule bounds via `hp`, `hq`, `hp'`, `hq'`.
- **For `idealSpan_range_update_divByMonic`**:
  - Rewrite `v j %ₘ v i` as `v j - v i * (v j /ₘ v i)` (using `modByMonic_eq_sub_mul_div`).
  - Apply `Submodule.span_range_update_sub_smul`, which expresses that replacing an element by itself minus a multiple of another generator does not change the span.

- Quantitative analysis of polynomial division in non-field settings.
- Applications likely include:
  - Constructive ideal membership / Gröbner basis theory over rings.
  - Bounded complexity of division algorithms in algebraic geometry over spectra of rings.
  - Formalization of effective commutative algebra (e.g., in `Mathlib`’s `RingTheory/Noetherian` or `GroebnerBasis` modules).

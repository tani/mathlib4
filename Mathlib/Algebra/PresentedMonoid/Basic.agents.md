### Technical Metadata Brief: `PresentedMonoid` Construction in Lean 4

---

#### **1. Key Definitions & Theorems**

| Name | Type / Signature | Purpose |
|------|------------------|---------|
| `PresentedMonoid` | `FreeMonoid α → FreeMonoid α → Prop → Type*` | Constructs a monoid as the quotient of the free monoid on `α` by the congruence generated by a binary relation `rels`. |
| `mk` | `FreeMonoid α →* PresentedMonoid rels` | Canonical monoid homomorphism from the free monoid to the presented monoid (the quotient map). |
| `of` | `α → PresentedMonoid rels` | Canonical map sending each generator `x : α` to its equivalence class in the presented monoid. |
| `lift` | `(f : α → M) → (∀ a b, rels a b → FreeMonoid.lift f a = FreeMonoid.lift f b) → PresentedMonoid rels →* M` | Universal property: extends any function `f : α → M` satisfying the relations to a unique monoid homomorphism from the presented monoid. |
| `closure_range_of` | `Submonoid.closure (Set.range (of rels)) = ⊤` | The generators (via `of`) generate the entire presented monoid as a submonoid. |
| `surjective_mk` | `Function.Surjective (mk rels)` | The quotient map `mk` is surjective. |
| `ext` | `(∀ x, φ (of x) = ψ (of x)) → φ = ψ` | Extensionality: two homomorphisms out of a presented monoid are equal if they agree on generators. |
| `lift_of` | `lift f h (of x) = f x` | Compatibility of `lift` with `of`. |
| `toMonoid.unique` | Uniqueness of `lift`: any homomorphism agreeing with `f` on generators equals `lift f h`. |

---

#### **2. Naming Conventions**

- **Prefixes**:
  - `of`: canonical generator embedding.
  - `mk`: quotient map from free object.
  - `lift`: universal property extension.
  - `conGen`: congruence generation (from `Mathlib.GroupTheory.Congruence.Hom`).
- **Suffixes**:
  - `Monoid`: for multiplicative structures.
  - `AddMonoid`: for additive counterparts (via `to_additive`).
- **Pattern**:
  - `inductionOn`, `inductionOn₂`, `inductionOn₃`: elimination principles for quotient types.
  - `closure_range_of`, `surjective_mk`, `ext`: descriptive names reflecting mathematical content.

---

#### **3. Tactic Stack**

Frequently used tactics in proofs:
- `induction'` / `induction`: for structural induction on `FreeMonoid` or `PresentedMonoid`.
- `rw [Submonoid.eq_top_iff']`: to reduce submonoid equality to membership.
- `exact`, `intro`, `rename_i`, `exact?` (implicit in `induction`).
- `apply`, `apply_congr`, `ext`: for homomorphism extensionality and equality.
- `let_fun`: to introduce function extensionality hypotheses.
- `simp_rw` (via `to_additive` attributes): for automatic additive/multiplicative translation.

No heavy automation like `aesop` or `ring` is used—proofs rely on low-level quotient and congruence reasoning.

---

#### **4. Proof Logic**

- **Structure**: Most proofs follow a *quotient induction* pattern:
  1. Reduce to representatives using `Quotient.ind` (e.g., `inductionOn`).
  2. Prove properties for `mk a` by induction on `a : FreeMonoid α`.
  3. Use structural induction on `FreeMonoid` (cases: `1`, `mul`, `of`).
- **Universal Property**:
  - `lift` is defined via `Con.lift`, requiring a proof that `rels` are respected under `FreeMonoid.lift f`.
  - Uniqueness follows from `Con.lift_unique` and density of generators (`closure_range_of`).
- **Extensionality** (`ext`):
  - Uses `eq_on_denseM` (or `eq_on_range`), leveraging that generators densely generate the monoid.

---

#### **5. Imports & Scope**

- **Core Dependencies**:
  - `Mathlib.Algebra.FreeMonoid.Basic`: Free monoid construction and universal property.
  - `Mathlib.Algebra.Group.Submonoid.Operations`: Submonoid closure, `Submonoid.closure`, `eq_top_iff'`.
  - `Mathlib.GroupTheory.Congruence.Hom`: Congruences, `conGen`, `Con.lift`, `Quotient`-based constructions.

- **Scope**:  
  This module formalizes *monoid presentations* in the style of universal algebra:  
  > *Given generators `α` and relations `rels ⊆ FreeMonoid α × FreeMonoid α`, construct the initial monoid satisfying those relations.*

- **Additive Counterpart**:  
  All definitions and theorems have `to_additive` variants (e.g., `PresentedAddMonoid`, `add_of`, `add_lift`), indicating support for both multiplicative and additive notation.

### Summary

This file provides a foundational, constructive treatment of monoids defined by generators and relations, leveraging Lean’s quotient and congruence infrastructure. It emphasizes *universal properties* and *induction principles*, aligning with categorical and algebraic conventions. The design is modular, reusable, and compatible with Mathlib’s additive/multiplicative duality via `to_additive`.

### Technical Brief: `FiniteProd.lean`

---

#### **1. Key Definitions & Theorems**

| Name | Type / Signature | Purpose |
|------|------------------|---------|
| `iSup_subcomplexOfSimplex_prod_eq_top` | `⨆ (x₁ : X₁.N) (x₂ : X₂.N), (Subcomplex.ofSimplex x₁.simplex).prod (Subcomplex.ofSimplex x₂.simplex) = ⊤` | Shows that the supremum of products of principal subcomplexes (generated by single simplices) covers the entire product simplicial set. |
| `Subcomplex.ofSimplexProd_eq_range` | `(Subcomplex.ofSimplex x₁).prod (Subcomplex.ofSimplex x₂) = Subcomplex.range (yonedaEquiv.symm x₁ ⊗ₘ yonedaEquiv.symm x₂)` | Identifies the product of principal subcomplexes with the range of the tensor product of representing maps. |
| `hasDimensionLT_prod` | `(X₁ ⊗ X₂).HasDimensionLT n` under `X₁.HasDimensionLT d₁`, `X₂.HasDimensionLT d₂`, and `d₁ + d₂ ≤ n + 1` | Proves that the dimension of the product is bounded by the sum of dimensions (strict inequality version). |
| `hasDimensionLE_prod` | `(X₁ ⊗ X₂).HasDimensionLE n` under `X₁.HasDimensionLE d₁`, `X₂.HasDimensionLE d₂`, and `d₁ + d₂ ≤ n` | Non-strict version of the dimension bound. |
| `instance [Finite X₁] [Finite X₂] : Finite (X₁ ⊗ X₂)` | `Finite (X₁ ⊗ X₂)` | Shows finiteness is preserved under binary product. |
| `finite_of_isPullback` | `IsPullback t l r b → X₂.Finite → X₃.Finite → X₁.Finite` | Finiteness descends along pullbacks when one leg is monic (used for pullback stability). |
| `instance (r : X₂ ⟶ X₄) (b : X₃ ⟶ X₄) : Finite (pullback r b)` | `Finite (pullback r b)` | Pullbacks of finite simplicial sets are finite. |

#### **2. Naming Conventions**

- **Prefixes**:
  - `hasDimensionLT_`, `hasDimensionLE_`: indicate dimension bounds (strict/non-strict).
  - `finite_of_`: constructs finiteness from structural properties (e.g., mono, pullback).
  - `iSup_`, `ofSimplex_`, `ofSimplexProd_`: refer to constructions via suprema or principal subcomplexes.
- **Suffixes**:
  - `_eq_top`: asserts equality with the top subcomplex.
  - `_eq_range`: identifies a subcomplex as a range.
  - `_prod`: refers to binary product constructions.

#### **4. Proof Logic**

- **Structure**:
  1. **Decomposition**: Express the product as a supremum of products of principal subcomplexes (`iSup_subcomplexOfSimplex_prod_eq_top`).
  2. **Identification**: Replace each product of principal subcomplexes with a range (`Subcomplex.ofSimplexProd_eq_range`).
  3. **Dimension Analysis**: Use known dimension bounds on `X₁`, `X₂`, and the tensor product of standard simplices (`Δ[p] ⊗ Δ[q]`) to bound the dimension of each component.
  4. **Supremum Argument**: Apply `hasDimensionLT_iSup_iff` to lift the bound to the whole product.
  5. **Finiteness**: Combine dimension bound with `finite_of_hasDimensionLT`.
  6. **Pullback Stability**: Use monicity of the lift in a pullback square to descend finiteness.

- **Induction**: Not used directly; relies on structural properties of subcomplexes and dimension axioms.

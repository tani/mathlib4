### **1. Key Definitions & Theorems**

| Name | Type / Structure | Purpose |
|------|------------------|---------|
| `RelativeMorphism A B φ` | `Structure` | Represents morphisms $X \to Y$ in $\mathbf{SSet}$ that extend a given morphism $\varphi : A \to B$ between subcomplexes $A \leq X$, $B \leq Y$. |
| `Homotopy f g` | `Structure` | A homotopy between two relative morphisms $f, g : \mathrm{RelativeMorphism}\ A\ B\ \varphi$, relative to $A, B$. |
| `HomotopyClass A B φ` | `Type u` | Quotient of $\mathrm{RelativeMorphism}\ A\ B\ \varphi$ by homotopy equivalence. |
| `RelativeMorphism.const` | `def` | Constant relative morphism when target subcomplex is generated by a $0$-simplex. |
| `RelativeMorphism.ofSimplex₀` | `def` | Pointed morphism $(X,x) \to (Y,y)$ induced by a map $f : X \to Y$ with $f(x)=y$. |
| `RelativeMorphism.comp` | `def` | Composition of relative morphisms, compatible with the mediating map $\varphi \circ \psi = \varphi\psi$. |
| `Homotopy.postcomp` | `def` | Postcomposition of a homotopy with a relative morphism. |
| `Homotopy.precomp` | `def` | Precomposition of a homotopy with a relative morphism. |
| `HomotopyClass.postcomp` / `precomp` | `def` | Induced operations on homotopy classes, well-defined via homotopy invariance. |
| `Homotopy.ofEq`, `Homotopy.refl` | `def` | Reflexivity and equality-induced homotopies. |
| `Homotopy.eq` | `lemma` | Equality of homotopy classes follows from existence of a homotopy. |
| `HomotopyClass.eq_homotopyClass` | `lemma` | Surjectivity of the quotient map. |

### **2. Naming Conventions**

- **Prefixes**:
  - `RelativeMorphism.`: for structures/defs/lemmas about relative morphisms.
  - `Homotopy.`: for homotopy-related constructions.
  - `homotopyClass`: for the quotient map.
- **Suffixes**:
  - `_class`: for quotient constructions (`HomotopyClass`).
  - `_homotopyClass`: for lemmas about interaction with `homotopyClass`.
  - `postcomp`, `precomp`: for whiskering/composition actions.
- **Structure fields**:
  - `map`, `h`, `h₀`, `h₁`, `rel`, `comm`: standard categorical/ simplicial notation.

---

### **3. Tactic Stack**

- **`cat_disch`**: used to discharge category-theoretic commutativity goals (via `reassoc` and `simp`).
- **`simp` / `simp_rw`**: heavily used for simplification of whiskering, associativity, and simplicial identities.
- **`aesop`**: used in `image_le` to automate reasoning about subcomplex inclusions.
- **`congr_fun`, `congr_app`**: for extensionality arguments in simplicial sets.
- **`rw`, `apply`, `intro`**: standard Lean proof tactics.

---

### **4. Proof Logic**

- **Induction / Extensionality**: Proofs often rely on extensionality of simplicial maps (`ext` attribute on structures), and on universal properties of subcomplexes and tensor products.
- **Quotient reasoning**: Homotopy classes are defined via `Quot`, and well-definedness of induced operations uses `Quot.lift` with proofs that homotopies are preserved.
- **Diagram chasing**: Many proofs (e.g., `Homotopy.precomp`, `Homotopy.postcomp`) involve manipulating whiskering and associativity using `simp` with `reassoc` hints.
- **Subcomplex calculus**: Lemmas like `image_le`, `le_preimage` use subcomplex inclusion properties and `Subcomplex.image_le_iff`.

### **5. Imports**

- `Mathlib.AlgebraicTopology.SimplicialSet.Monoidal`: for monoidal structure on $\mathbf{SSet}$, especially the tensor product $\otimes$ and $\Delta[1]$.
- `Mathlib.AlgebraicTopology.SimplicialSet.Subcomplex`: for subcomplexes, inclusions $\iota : A \hookrightarrow X$, image/preimage operations.
